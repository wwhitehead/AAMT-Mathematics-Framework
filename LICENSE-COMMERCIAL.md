## 1. Which licence applies to which work

This series has been published under two different licences at different times,
and the distinction is permanent. **Check the licence line in the header of the
specific paper you are reading.** It governs that paper.

### 1.1 Works published under CC BY 4.0 — irrevocably

The following were deposited on Zenodo and published on GitHub under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**:

- Foundations Papers I–V
- Working Papers WP-01 through WP-25
- Experiments EXP-01 and EXP-02

CC BY 4.0 is a **perpetual, irrevocable** grant. For the versions already
published, it permits commercial use, adaptation, and redistribution by anyone,
subject only to attribution. That grant cannot be withdrawn, and nothing in this
document attempts to withdraw it. Later revisions of these papers may carry a
different licence; the already-published versions do not.

If you are using one of those versions, your obligation is attribution as
described in §3.

### 1.2 Works published under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

WP-26 and later, and any revision that says so in its own header, are licensed
under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0
International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)**.

Under that licence you **may**, at no cost:

- read, download, and store the work;
- redistribute it unmodified, with attribution;
- quote from it in academic and journalistic work with citation;
- implement the ideas it describes in your own independent code.

You **may not**, without a separate written licence:

- use the work or any substantial part of it for commercial purposes;
- publish adaptations, translations, or derivative texts;
- redistribute modified versions.

### 1.3 Source code

Code in the AsAManThinks platform repositories is **not** covered by either
Creative Commons licence. Creative Commons explicitly recommends against using
its licences for software. Code is proprietary and all rights are reserved
unless a specific file or package states otherwise. See §5.

  work;
- republication in any paid medium, including paid courses and books.

### 2.1 What a paid licence grants

The default terms (CC BY-NC-ND) are the restrictive ones. A paid licence lifts
those restrictions for the licensee — it is the *open* version of these terms,
granted on payment rather than to the public. Depending on what is agreed, it
may grant any of:

| Right | Under CC BY-NC-ND | Under a paid licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read, store, redistribute unmodified | yes | yes |
| Quote with citation | yes | yes |
| Commercial use | **no** | negotiable — yes |
| Adaptations, translations, derivative texts | **no** | negotiable — yes |
| Redistribute modified versions | **no** | negotiable — yes |
| Train / fine-tune / evaluate an ML system | **no** | negotiable — yes |
| Remove attribution | no | no |
| Use the trademarks | no | separate agreement |

Attribution is never waived. A paid licence removes restrictions on *use*; it
does not transfer authorship or permit the work to be presented as the
licensee's own.

### 2.2 Shape of the terms

Terms are set per engagement rather than published as a price list, because the
relevant variables differ by licensee — scope of works, whether derivatives are
needed, whether ML training is in scope, term length, and whether the licence is
exclusive in any field. Common structures include a one-off fee for a fixed
scope, an annual licence for ongoing use, and royalty or revenue-share
arrangements where the work is central to a commercial product.

Academic institutions, independent researchers, and non-commercial open-source
projects should not need to pay for anything: CC BY-NC-ND already covers that
use, and where it does not quite fit, ask — those requests are generally granted
at no cost.

For any use of any paper in this series, under either licence:

endorsement of your work by the author. Removing or obscuring the author

## 4. Machine learning, text and data mining

**Use of these works to train, fine-tune, evaluate, benchmark, or ground a
machine-learning model — including large language models and retrieval systems —
is a use for which a separate written licence is required, and is not granted
by CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 where the resulting system is commercial.**

This is stated explicitly rather than left to inference because the question is
live, unsettled in most jurisdictions, and the works in this series describe
methods that are directly useful to model builders. An express reservation is
made here of any text-and-data-mining rights that applicable law permits to be
reserved, including under Article 4(3) of EU Directive 2019/790.

Note the limit of this: for the CC BY 4.0 works in §1.1, that grant already
permits commercial use, and this reservation cannot narrow it retroactively.

## 6. Enforcement and good faith

The purpose of this series is public record and open scholarship. The purpose of
the restrictions is that the author be credited and, where the work creates
commercial value for someone else, compensated. Those goals are compatible with
generous academic use and this document is not intended to discourage it.

If you are unsure whether your use requires a licence, ask. A short email is
cheaper for both parties than the alternative.
